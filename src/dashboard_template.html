Credit Risk Analyst

Ask about the filtered portfolio's defaults, PD, RWA, EAD, or sector and vintage comparisons. Uses local rules; no API key or paid AI service.

Default Rate by Sector

Meaning: the share of loans marked defaulted in each sector. Why it matters: highlights where realized defaults are concentrated; compare loan counts and do not infer the cause from the rate alone.

Vintage Default Rate

Meaning: the observed default share for loans grouped by origination year. Why it matters: helps compare cohorts, but newer vintages may have had less time to default.

PD Distribution

Meaning: distribution of the source annual probability-of-default estimates across filtered loans. Why it matters: shows the portfolio's modeled risk mix and high-PD tail. It is an estimate, not a count of actual defaults.

Highest Annual PD Review List

Meaning: up to 20 filtered loans with the highest source annual PD. Why it matters: provides a review queue for checking risk assumptions and exposure; it is not a lending decision or a forecast of certain default.

{pd_spikes_table}

RWA vs Unexpected Loss

Meaning: each point is a loan's source RWA and source unexpected-loss value. Why it matters: helps inspect how the two supplied risk measures vary together. The dataset does not specify their currency or calculation method, and association does not prove causation.

Highest RWA-to-EAD Ratios Review List

Meaning: ranks source RWA divided by exposure at default (EAD). Why it matters: puts RWA in exposure-relative context; a high ratio is a screening cue, not an OSFI risk weight validation.

{rwa_jump_table}

Initial Credit Rating Distribution

Meaning: counts the source initial-rating categories. Why it matters: shows the rating mix used to describe portfolio composition. These categories are not IFRS 9 stages and do not show rating migration.

Largest Expected Loss Exposures

Meaning: ranks loans by the dataset's expected-loss (EL) field, which closely follows PD × LGD × EAD. Why it matters: identifies where modeled expected loss is concentrated for portfolio review; it is not a decision about an individual borrower.

Selected Loan Details

PD is probability of default, LGD is loss severity if default occurs, EAD is exposure at default, EL is expected loss, and RWA is the source risk-weighted-asset value. These are dataset fields; the dashboard does not validate individual credit decisions.

Expected Loss Concentration by Sector

Meaning: sum of source expected loss by sector. Why it matters: shows which sectors contribute the largest modeled loss amounts; compare with exposure sizes and default rates.

Average Annual PD and Observed Defaults by Vintage

Meaning: compares average source annual PD with the historical default share for each origination year. Why it matters: can guide model-performance review, but valid calibration requires a defined and comparable observation horizon.

Stress Testing Summary

Meaning: applies the supplied scenario file's sector-level PD multipliers and LGD assumptions to the filtered loans. Why it matters: illustrates sensitivity of expected loss to those assumptions; this is not an OSFI-prescribed stress test or regulatory capital result.
