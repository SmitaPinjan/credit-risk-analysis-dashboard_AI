Meaning: the observed default share for loans grouped by origination year. Why it matters: helps compare cohorts, but newer vintages may have had less time to default.

PD Distribution

Meaning: distribution of the source annual probability-of-default estimates across filtered loans. Why it matters: shows the portfolio's modeled risk mix and high-PD tail. It is an estimate, not a count of actual defaults.

Meaning: sum of source expected loss by sector. Why it matters: shows which sectors contribute the largest modeled loss amounts; compare with exposure sizes and default rates.

Meaning: compares average source annual PD with the historical default share for each origination year. Why it matters: can guide model-performance review, but valid calibration requires a defined and comparable observation horizon.
